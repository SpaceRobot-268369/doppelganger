# Code of Conduct

We want Doppelganger to be a respectful, practical, and safe open-source
community. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, welcome different
experience levels, and give actionable technical feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be handled
with discretion and without retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This concise policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
